# Terms of Service

The Montage Subtitle Translator is a free, open-source subtitle translation service developed and maintained by the **MontageSubs** community.

MontageSubs is a non-profit community of film and TV enthusiasts, subtitlers, industry professionals, and developers. We share a passion for film and subtitling, and believe the knowledge and tools behind the craft should be accessible to everyone. Through open-source collaboration, we aim to foster cross-language understanding and cultural exchange.

We know that free services still have costs. This project brings together community contributions and expertise in subtitle formats, typesetting, and natural language processing. Our goal is to make these tools available to everyone through a reliable public service.

To keep the service running, we ask all users to follow a few guidelines that help protect shared resources and support the community.

## 1. Our Philosophy & Operating Model

This service is maintained by community members who volunteer their time and resources. Infrastructure and translation API costs are covered by personal contributions from maintainers, free API quotas, and other community resources.

We want everyone to benefit from the tools and knowledge we've made available:

**For Subtitlers**: Features such as glossaries, context awareness, scene recognition, SDH styling, bold styling, and ASS style settings help you produce high-quality machine translation drafts, with additional processing through local models. The built-in editor, comparison preview, and automatic error detection also make proofreading easier. We welcome you to join the community and share your experience with fellow subtitlers.

**For Film and TV Enthusiasts**: Get translated subtitles with professional formatting and accurate timing in seconds. Bilingual subtitles also make the tool useful for language learning. We hope it sparks your interest in subtitling and encourages you to try making subtitles yourself.

**For Developers**: Explore the source code to see how subtitle formats and styling rules are handled in practice. You're welcome to join the community, contribute to projects, and help bring new ideas to life.

**For Filmmakers and Content Creators**: We aim to support independent creators with translation and subtitling resources, helping their work reach audiences across language barriers.

If you'd like to help the MontageSubs community grow, visit our [Contribute](../contribute/en.md) page to share feedback, contribute code, or volunteer your time.

## 3. Acceptable Use

To keep the service available and fair for everyone, all users must follow these guidelines:

**1. Use the Service Only for Permitted Content**

Do not use this service to translate illegal, infringing, or malicious content, or content that could jeopardize the service itself. We rely on multiple translation providers, including local and upstream models, so you must also comply with their respective terms and policies. Even if content is legal in your jurisdiction, do not submit it if doing so would violate the laws or policies applicable to our providers.

**Our Core Principle**: If you suspect that content may be harmful or legally questionable, do not process it through this service. Violations could lead to service shutdowns or stricter restrictions, affecting the entire community.

**2. Respect Shared Resources & Quotas**
*   **Rate Limits**: We use dynamic quotas to distribute resources fairly. Subtitle translation often involves processing multiple segments in succession, and hitting a limit mid-task can interrupt your work. To help subtitlers finish ongoing tasks, we provide an additional quota allowance the first time a user reaches a rate limit. This is intended to help complete the current task, not to bypass usage restrictions. Attempts to circumvent rate limits, exploit vulnerabilities, or abuse this allowance are prohibited. If you reach a limit, please wait a while; restrictions generally lift automatically.

**3. Do Not Abuse Shared Resources**

This service is intended for people who genuinely use and care about subtitling, film, and television. We allow reasonable browser extensions and accessibility tools, but prohibit unauthorized bulk requests, unattended automated calls, and other forms of resource abuse. Such activity can quickly exhaust shared resources and disrupt access for everyone.

**4. Protect the Community**
*   **Infrastructure Security**: Do not attempt unauthorized attacks or probes against our infrastructure, service interfaces, or providers. We welcome responsible security research. If you discover a potential vulnerability, please follow our [Security Vulnerability Reporting Guide](../feedback/en.md) to report it.
*   **Respect for Others**: Harassment and personal attacks against community members or contributors are not tolerated. We follow the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) to maintain a respectful and supportive environment.

## 4. Enforcement & Appeals

Depending on the severity of a violation, we may:

1. Issue a reminder or warning;
2. Temporarily or permanently restrict access to the service;
3. Take legal action in cases involving malicious attacks or serious unlawful activity.

**Appeals**: Automated restrictions can occasionally produce false positives. If you believe your access has been restricted by mistake, contact us through our community groups or discussion forums. We'll help investigate the issue and review your appeal.
