# Terms of Service

The Montage Subtitle Translator is a free, open-source subtitle translation service developed and maintained by the **MontageSubs** community.

MontageSubs is a non-profit community of film and TV enthusiasts, subtitlers, industry professionals, and developers. We share a passion for film and subtitling, and believe the knowledge and tools behind the craft should be accessible to everyone. Through open-source collaboration, we aim to encourage cross-language understanding and cultural exchange while building an open and thriving subtitle ecosystem.

We know that free services still come at a cost. This project brings together contributions from community members, combining practical expertise in subtitle formats, typesetting, and natural language processing. Our aim is to make these resources available to everyone through a reliable, high-quality public service.

To keep the service sustainable, we ask everyone to follow a few shared guidelines that protect our limited resources and help keep the community healthy.

## 1. Our Philosophy & Operating Model

This service is maintained through the time and effort of community members. Infrastructure and translation API costs are covered by personal contributions from maintainers, free API quotas, and other community resources.

We want everyone to benefit from open-source tools and free public services:

**For Subtitlers**: Advanced features such as glossaries, context awareness, scene recognition, SDH styling, bold styling, and ASS style settings help you produce high-quality machine translation drafts, with optional processing through local models. The built-in editor, comparison preview, and automatic error detection also make proofreading more efficient. We welcome you to join the community, share your experience, and exchange ideas with fellow subtitlers.

**For Film and TV Enthusiasts**: Get professionally formatted translations with accurate timing in seconds. Whether you're following foreign-language content or learning a language with bilingual subtitles, the tool makes it easier to enjoy what you're watching. We also hope it sparks an interest in subtitling and encourages you to try making subtitles yourself.

**For Developers**: Explore the source code to see how subtitle formats, typesetting, and technical standards are handled in practice. Join the community, work on interesting projects, and help turn good ideas into reality.

**For Filmmakers and Content Creators**: We aim to support independent creators with language and subtitling resources, helping their work reach audiences beyond language barriers.

We want everyone to get something useful out of this service and help the MontageSubs community grow. If you share our vision, visit our [Contribute] page to learn how to get involved, whether by sharing feedback, contributing code, or volunteering your time.

## 2. Licensing & Resource Stewardship

**The Web Tool**: The web tool is available without an account or registration, and contains no ads or tracking. Our privacy measures are designed to protect your information. See our [Privacy Policy] for details.

**The Source Code**: This project is released under the MIT License. Its source code, including subtitle processing logic and optimization algorithms, is open to the public. You're welcome to use, modify, and contribute to it.

**The Public Backend**: We encourage developers to build on our open-source code. However, the hosted backend and API quotas are limited resources shared by the community. To ensure fair access and keep the service running reliably, public access is currently available only through the official web interface. Third-party clients cannot connect directly to our public APIs, as uncontrolled usage could exhaust shared resources and disrupt the service for everyone.

If you have a legitimate integration need, please contact us through GitHub Issues or Discussions. We're open to discussing workable solutions that preserve fair access to shared resources.

## 3. Acceptable Use

To keep this service sustainable and fair for everyone, all users are expected to follow these guidelines:

**1. Use the Service for Permitted Content**

Do not use this service to translate illegal, infringing, or malicious content, or anything that could jeopardize the continued operation of the service. We rely on multiple translation providers, including local and upstream models, so you must also comply with their respective terms and policies. Content that is legal in your jurisdiction may still violate the laws or policies applicable to a service provider. Do not submit such content.

**Our Core Principle**: If you suspect that content may be harmful or legally questionable, do not process it through this service. Violations could result in service shutdowns or stricter restrictions, affecting the entire community.

**2. Respect Shared Resources & Quotas**
*   **Rate Limits**: We use a dynamic quota system to distribute resources fairly. Subtitle work often involves processing multiple segments in succession, and hitting a limit mid-task can be disruptive. To help subtitlers finish their current work, we provide an additional quota allowance the first time a user reaches a rate limit. This allowance is intended to help complete ongoing tasks, not to bypass usage restrictions. Attempts to circumvent rate limits, exploit vulnerabilities, or abuse this additional allowance are prohibited. If you reach a limit, please take a break. Restrictions generally lift automatically after some time.

**3. Do Not Abuse Shared Resources**

This service is intended for people who genuinely use and care about subtitling and film. We allow reasonable browser extensions and accessibility tools that improve the user experience, but prohibit unauthorized bulk requests, unattended automated calls, and other forms of resource abuse.

Such activity can quickly exhaust shared resources and affect everyone's access. Automated scripts also lack the human judgment needed to handle linguistic nuance and do not contribute to the knowledge-sharing that helps the community improve.

**4. Protect the Community**
*   **Infrastructure Security**: Do not attempt unauthorized attacks or probes against our infrastructure, service interfaces, or providers. We welcome responsible security research. If you discover a potential vulnerability, please follow our [Security Vulnerability Reporting Guide] to report it.
*   **Respect for Others**: Harassment and personal attacks against community members or contributors are not tolerated. We follow the [Contributor Covenant] to maintain a respectful and supportive environment.

## 4. Enforcement & Appeals

Depending on the severity of a violation, we may take the following actions:

1. Issue a friendly reminder or warning;
2. Temporarily or permanently restrict access to the service;
3. Take legal action in cases involving malicious attacks or serious unlawful activity.

**Appeals**: Automated restrictions can occasionally produce false positives. If you believe your access has been restricted by mistake, contact us through our community groups or discussion forums. We'll help investigate the issue and review your appeal.
