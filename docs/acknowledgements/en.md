## Projects and Models

The subtitle word lists that speed up name and term recognition are built from these open-source projects:

| Project | Made by | License | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [stopwords-iso](https://github.com/stopwords-iso/stopwords-iso) | stopwords-iso | MIT | Common words in many languages |
| [most-common-words-by-language](https://www.npmjs.com/package/most-common-words-by-language) | Its maintainers | MIT | Word frequency lists |
| [FrequencyWords](https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords) | hermitdave, from OpenSubtitles data | CC BY-SA 4.0 | The frequency data behind the lists above |
| [stdlib first-name datasets](https://github.com/stdlib-js/stdlib) | stdlib | Apache-2.0 | English first names |

The local recognition models you can download in the app come from these developers:

| Model | Made by | License | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [BERT multilingual NER](https://huggingface.co/Xenova/bert-base-multilingual-cased-ner-hrl) | Davlan, converted for the browser by Xenova | AFL-3.0 | Recognizing names in many languages |
| [DistilBERT multilingual NER](https://huggingface.co/Xenova/distilbert-base-multilingual-cased-ner-hrl) | Davlan, converted for the browser by Xenova | AFL-3.0 | A lighter option for the same languages |
| [XLM-RoBERTa Japanese NER](https://huggingface.co/jiting/xlm-roberta-ner-japanese_onnx) | tsmatz, converted for the browser by jiting | MIT | Recognizing names in Japanese |

## Organizations and Platforms

- [Weblate](https://hosted.weblate.org/projects/subtitle-translator/web-interface/) and [Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/montage-subtitle-translator) host our interface translations, so volunteers around the world can work on them together.
- [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/) hosts the models listed above.
- [GitHub](https://github.com/MontageSubs/subtitle-translator) and [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/) host the code, the site and its services.
