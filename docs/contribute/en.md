# Contribute

**Subtitle Translator** is completely open source and community-driven. We warmly welcome everyone passionate about subtitling, translation quality, frontend engineering, and UI/UX design to join us!

---

## How to Contribute

You can participate and contribute in various ways:

1. **Improve Translation & Glossaries**:
   - Suggest domain-specific terminology, idiom pairings, and slang translations.
   - Refine post-processing rules and line-breaking heuristics for subtitling.
2. **Code & Feature Contributions**:
   - Submit Pull Requests on [GitHub](https://github.com/MontageSubs/subtitle-translator) to fix bugs, optimize performance, or introduce new features.
3. **Report Issues & Provide Feedback**:
   - Encountered translation errors, formatting quirks, or UI glitches? [Report an issue](../docs/report-issue) or open a [GitHub Issue](https://github.com/MontageSubs/subtitle-translator/issues).
4. **Docs & Localization**:
   - Help translate and polish guides, privacy policies, and user manuals across different languages.

---

## Contributors

Thank you to everyone who has contributed to this project — through code, documentation, translation, design, or community support. Your efforts make better subtitles accessible to all!
