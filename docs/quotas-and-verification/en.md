# Quotas and Human Verification

Thanks for using Montage Subtitle Translator!

Montage Subtitle Translator is a free, open-source community service. There's no sign-up, and in most cases, you can use it without ever being asked to complete a human verification.

### Why am I asked for human verification?

We have limited resources to keep the service running, and every translation request uses some of that capacity. To make sure it stays available to everyone, we enforce fair-use quotas that adjust to overall demand.

If we detect an unusual spike in usage, we may ask you to complete a human verification (CAPTCHA) before continuing. This helps us distinguish normal use from automated traffic.

It's just a safeguard to protect the service. It doesn't mean you've done anything wrong.

### What does "The server is busy" mean?

This message usually means your quota for the moment has run out. Once the countdown ends, you can try again.

If you're partway through a translation, we may also offer an extra quota so that you can finish the task at hand. It's meant for exactly that: it can only be used when your regular quota runs out before the current task is done, and whether to use it is up to you. It resets at 00:00 UTC every day and doesn't carry over, so one that goes unused isn't saved for later.

Whether it's available depends on how busy the server is and how much quota has been used in the same period, so when demand is especially high it may not be offered. We provide it as a gesture of goodwill from a volunteer-run service, to help people finish what they started. It isn't part of the regular quota, so please don't count on it being there every time.

### Will I still be restricted after verifying?

In most cases, passing the verification is all you need to continue using the service.

Keep in mind that human verification is more likely when shared capacity is under pressure or your usage is unusually high. Please use the available quota considerately so others can access the service, too.

If usage remains excessive, we may temporarily suspend your access.

Suspension periods vary depending on available capacity, your recent usage, and overall demand. Access may return within a few hours, but in some cases it can take a day or longer.

Usage-related restrictions are temporary. You can use the service again once capacity becomes available.

We reserve the right to permanently revoke access if we confirm that someone is abusing the service through bots or automated scripts.

### Need more quota?

If you regularly need more capacity, for example, to translate large subtitle collections, join our [community](../community/en.md) and let us know. We can discuss what might be possible.

Please don't use scripts or bots to make bulk translation requests. The service is a shared community resource, and fair access depends on everyone using it responsibly.

For the full rules, see our [Terms of Service](../terms/en.md).

If you believe your access has been restricted by mistake, or you're having trouble with the verification, please get in touch through our [feedback page](../feedback/en.md).

Thanks for helping us keep the service available to everyone!

We hope Montage Subtitle Translator makes subtitle translation a little easier and helps more people enjoy content across languages and cultures.
