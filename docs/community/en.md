# Join the Community

Montage Subtitle Translator is built in the open by people who love subtitles, translation and film, and there's always room for one more. Got a question, an idea, or just a bit of curiosity about what we're up to? Come on in. You don't have to contribute anything to belong here, and lurking is a perfectly respectable way to take part.

## Where to Find Us

- [Telegram](https://t.me/+q3jiRo5uOGRkYmYx)
- [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/UuQ8tFpMYy)
- [IRC](https://web.libera.chat/#MontageSubs) (Libera.Chat, `#MontageSubs`)
- [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/MontageSubs/subtitle-translator/discussions)
- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/MontageSubs/subtitle-translator/issues)

Telegram, Discord and IRC are where the day-to-day chatter happens, so pop in and say hi, ask anything, or listen in for a while. GitHub Discussions suits questions and ideas worth keeping around for the next person, and GitHub Issues is for bug reports. Not sure where something belongs? Just ask in chat and we'll point you the right way. For what to include in a bug report, see [Feedback & Suggestions](../feedback/en.md).

## Reaching the Maintainers

The core maintainers and community admins hang out in these spaces and are happy to help. To reach them privately, join any of the chats above and send a direct message.

Security issues are the one exception to "ask anywhere". Please keep the details out of public channels, message a maintainer privately and follow the [Security Disclosure Guide](../security/en.md).

## Keeping It Friendly

We want these spaces to stay kind and patient, especially toward newcomers who are still finding their way around. Everyone here follows our [Code of Conduct](../code-of-conduct/en.md), which also explains how to report a problem.

## Want to Do More?

If the chat has you itching to pitch in, [How to Contribute](../contribute/en.md) lists plenty of ways, from code to translations. None of them require being an expert.
