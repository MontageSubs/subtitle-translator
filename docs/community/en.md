# Join the Community

Montage Subtitle Translator is built and maintained in the open by people who care about subtitles, translation, and film. Whether you want to ask a question, share an idea, or just see how things work, you're welcome to join us. You don't have to contribute anything to be part of the community.

## Where to Find Us

- [Telegram](https://t.me/+q3jiRo5uOGRkYmYx)
- [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/UuQ8tFpMYy)
- [IRC](https://web.libera.chat/#MontageSubs) (Libera.Chat, `#MontageSubs`)
- [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/MontageSubs/subtitle-translator/discussions)
- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/MontageSubs/subtitle-translator/issues)

Telegram, Discord, and IRC are for everyday conversation. GitHub Discussions is a good place for questions and ideas that others may want to find later, and GitHub Issues is for bug reports. See [Feedback & Suggestions](../feedback/en.md) for what to include when reporting a problem.

## Reaching the Maintainers

The project's core maintainers and community administrators look after these spaces. If you need to reach them privately, join any of the chats above and send them a direct message.

For security issues, please don't post details publicly. Message a maintainer privately and follow the [Security Disclosure Guide](../security/en.md).

## Keeping the Community Welcoming

We want these spaces to stay friendly and respectful. Everyone taking part is expected to follow our [Code of Conduct](../code-of-conduct/en.md), which also explains how to report a problem.

## More Ways to Get Involved

If you'd like to do more than chat, see [How to Contribute](../contribute/en.md).
