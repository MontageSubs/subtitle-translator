# Join the Community

Montage Subtitle Translator is built in the open by people who care about subtitles, translation and film. Whether you have a question, an idea, or simply want to see what we're working on, you're welcome here. You don't need to contribute anything to be part of the community, and following along is a perfectly good way to take part.

## Where to Find Us

- [Telegram](https://t.me/+q3jiRo5uOGRkYmYx)
- [Discord](https://discord.com/invite/UuQ8tFpMYy)
- [IRC](https://web.libera.chat/#MontageSubs) (Libera.Chat, `#MontageSubs`)
- [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/MontageSubs/subtitle-translator/discussions)
- [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/MontageSubs/subtitle-translator/issues)

Telegram, Discord and IRC are where day-to-day conversation happens. Feel free to introduce yourself, ask a question, or follow along for a while. GitHub Discussions suits questions and ideas that are worth keeping for the next person, and GitHub Issues is for bug reports. If you're not sure where something belongs, ask in chat and we'll point you in the right direction. For what to include in a bug report, see [Feedback & Suggestions](../feedback/en.md).

## Reaching the Maintainers

The core maintainers and community admins are active in these spaces and are happy to help. To reach them privately, join any of the chats above and send a direct message.

Security issues are the one exception to "ask anywhere". Please keep the details out of public channels, message a maintainer privately and follow the [Security Disclosure Guide](../security/en.md).

## Keeping It Friendly

We want these spaces to stay kind and patient, especially toward newcomers who are still finding their way around. Everyone here follows our [Code of Conduct](../code-of-conduct/en.md), which also explains how to report a problem.

## Want to Do More?

If you'd like to get more involved, [How to Contribute](../contribute/en.md) lists many ways to help, from code to translation. None of them require you to be an expert.
